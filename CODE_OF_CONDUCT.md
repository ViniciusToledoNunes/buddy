# Code of conduct

This project is small, and the bar is simple: be decent to the people you are talking to.

**Expected:** discuss the code and the problem, assume the other person is acting in good faith, accept that a
maintainer may say no, and take a "no" without turning it into an argument about the person.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, sexualised language or imagery, sustained
disruption, or publishing anyone's private information without their permission.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space connected to the project.

**Reporting.** If someone behaves this way, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub
([@ViniciusToledoNunes](https://github.com/ViniciusToledoNunes)). Reports are handled discreetly. The maintainer
may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts, and will say when they do.
